A node is one running engine. It is live while its heartbeats keep arriving within the heartbeat timeout. Each piece of work that only one node may do at a time, such as delivering the messages in a queue, is a workload. Rendezvous hashing chooses the node that owns each workload from the live nodes, so when a node stops, its workloads move to the others.
