A queue holds commands and deadlines that have not yet been delivered. Its ID is the key of the workload of delivering its messages, so one node delivers them. A message that is due has reached its next attempt time. A message that is failing has failed at least once in a row. The error text of a failure is in the engine's log, not in the database.
